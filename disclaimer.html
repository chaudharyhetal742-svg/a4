Regulatory Disclosure

Product & Flight Disclaimer

Last updated: September 10, 2026 | Effective for all patrons and visitors of corduroyflight.com

1. General Educational Purpose of Aviation Historical Content

The historical articles, military outerwear analyses, flight jacket structural diagrams, and textile engineering treatises published across corduroyflight.com are curated exclusively for general educational, historical, and aesthetic appreciation. While our editorial contributors and master tailors conduct extensive archival research into twentieth-century military specifications, nothing published on this website constitutes formal flight instruction, aeronautical engineering certification, or military survival doctrine.

Aviation enthusiasts, student pilots, and outdoor adventurers must always consult official Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) manuals, certified flight instructors, and formal flight training operating handbooks for regulatory safety protocols. The historical narratives shared in our Flight Journal celebrate the rich visual and technical heritage of open-cockpit aviation, but should never be substituted for formal pilot training, cockpit safety checklists, or certified aircraft operational manuals under real-world flying conditions.

2. Nature of Outerwear Products and Non-Survival Equipment Disclosure

CorduroyFlight designs and handcrafts premium lifestyle and pilot outerwear engineered for comfort, warmth, wind resistance, and enduring sartorial distinction in cold urban and backcountry environments. Although our flight jackets are constructed to heavy military-grade benchmarks using 480 GSM cotton corduroy, genuine Australian shearling, and heavy-duty brass hardware, they are commercial heritage garments rather than certified personal protective equipment (PPE) or aviation survival suits.

Our outerwear is not certified, tested, or rated for military ejection, high-altitude cabin depressurization survival, open-ocean immersion protection, or extreme polar mountaineering above hazardous sub-zero survival thresholds. Patrons engaging in hazardous flight operations, experimental aviation, or extreme arctic wilderness expeditions must equip themselves with specialized, certified aviation survival suits, floatation devices, and flame-retardant flight suits designed specifically to meet statutory military life-support standards.

3. Weather Resistance, DWR Performance, and Natural Fiber Limitations

Our heavy-wale corduroy jackets feature an eco-conscious durable water-repellent (DWR) surface treatment designed to cause light rain showers, misty mountain fog, and dry snowfall to bead and roll effortlessly off the standing cotton pile. However, cotton is inherently a natural cellulose fiber, and corduroy is not a seam-sealed waterproof hardshell engineered for prolonged exposure to torrential downpours, severe squalls, or heavy aquatic submersions.

In prolonged heavy precipitation, moisture will eventually saturate the outer cotton fibers and may penetrate to the internal Primaloft insulation if worn without an appropriate waterproof technical umbrella or rain shell. CorduroyFlight explicitly disclaims liability for garment saturation, water damage to personal electronics stored in jacket pockets, or hypothermia risks incurred when patrons subject lifestyle corduroy outerwear to severe weather conditions beyond its intended functional design parameters.

4. Wear-and-Tear Realities, Patina Evolution, and Maintenance Bounds

Every corduroy flight jacket is designed to age with distinguished grace, developing a soft, vintage patina along seams, cuffs, elbows, and pocket flaps that mirrors the unique life, adventures, and posture of its owner. This gradual color softening, subtle nap wear, and brass hardware oxidation are celebrated hallmarks of authentic natural materials rather than manufacturing flaws, textile breakdowns, or latent material defects.

Garment longevity depends entirely upon proper patron care, appropriate seasonal storage, and the avoidance of harsh abrasive friction or improper laundry methods. Our published Martindale abrasion ratings and tensile laboratory metrics reflect controlled laboratory evaluations and do not constitute an unconditional warranty against fabric tearing, shearling shedding, or zipper binding caused by snagging against sharp metal edges, pet damage, chemical exposure, or unapproved laundering.

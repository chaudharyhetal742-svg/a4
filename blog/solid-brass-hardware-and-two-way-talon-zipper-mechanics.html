Hardware Metallurgy & Tailoring

Solid Brass Hardware and Two-Way Talon Zipper Mechanics

By Julian Vance • September 2026 • 10 Min Read • Verified Technical Archive

1. Metallurgy of Aviation Zippers: Why Solid Brass Outperforms Synthetic Coils

In high-performance outerwear, the front closure zipper is the single most vulnerable mechanical component. While modern mass-market apparel relies on lightweight extruded nylon coil zippers, aviation specifications have demanded solid metal tooth closures for nearly a century. Synthetic nylon coils soften and deform under heat, become brittle and snap in freezing sub-zero temperatures, and jam permanently when grit or dust enters the tooth track.

CorduroyFlight jackets feature custom #10 solid cast brass two-way zippers. Brass—an alloy of copper and zinc—possesses unique metallurgical properties ideal for aviation environments. It is completely non-ferrous and impervious to rust, resists galvanic corrosion when exposed to saltwater fog, and has a low coefficient of friction that allows the slider to glide smoothly without lubricating oils.

2. The Two-Way Slider Mechanism: Seated Cockpit Ergonomics

A major design defect in standard jackets is the single-slider zipper. When an individual sits down in an automobile, airplane seat, or desk chair, the rigid front zipper acts as a bowstring, forcing the lower front of the jacket to bunch upward against the chest and stomach.

Our heavy flight jackets are equipped with dual two-way sliders. By pulling the bottom slider upward two to four inches when seated, the lower hem flares naturally around the hips and thighs, eliminating bunching, preventing zipper wave distortion, and allowing effortless access to trouser pockets.

3. Pull Tab Ergonomics: Glove-Operated Leather Pulls

Pilots and outdoor workers frequently wear heavy insulated gloves in winter weather. Small metal zipper tabs are virtually impossible to grasp with gloved hands. Every CorduroyFlight zipper is fitted with an elongated 3-inch bridle leather pull tab, secured through the slider eyelet with a hand-stitched bar tack. This allows positive, tactile zipper operation in freezing conditions.

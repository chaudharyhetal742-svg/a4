Legal & Privacy Standards

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026 | Effective for all patrons and visitors of corduroyflight.com

1. Comprehensive Privacy Overview and Commitment

CorduroyFlight ("we," "our," or "the Atelier") operates the digital portal at corduroyflight.com to showcase our bespoke heavy-wale corduroy outerwear, aviation flight jackets, and precision tailoring archives. We are firmly committed to upholding the highest standards of data protection, digital privacy, and personal confidentiality for every patron, customer, and site visitor who interacts with our Mercer Street atelier. This Privacy Policy details the exact mechanisms through which personal information is collected, processed, safeguarded, and eventually archived or deleted across our web services.

When you browse our online collections, commission a custom aviator jacket, or correspond with our tailoring concierge, we believe you have an absolute right to know how your information is handled. We do not engage in aggressive commercial data harvesting, predatory tracking routines, or intrusive third-party profiling. Every piece of information gathered is collected solely to provide an elevated bespoke customer experience, facilitate accurate outerwear sizing calibrations, and ensure the seamless operational delivery of our handcrafted garments to your doorstep worldwide.

2. Categories of Information Collected by Our Systems

Our web infrastructure collects information through two transparent pathways: voluntarily provided personal correspondence and automated technical request telemetry. Voluntarily provided data includes your full name, shipping and billing postal addresses, direct telephone contact numbers, and electronic mail coordinates submitted when inquiring about flight jacket availability, booking showroom appointments, or subscribing to our quarterly Flight Dispatch newsletter. Without these verified details, our master tailors cannot accurately process your bespoke sizing specifications or arrange secure insured courier transit.

Automated technical request information is standard across modern web architecture and is captured instantaneously by our secure server logs whenever a browser requests an asset from our domain. This telemetry includes your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, operating system build, browser user-agent specification, referring universal resource locator, display viewport dimensions, and granular request timestamps. This non-personally identifiable telemetry allows our engineering team to diagnose server latency, defend against distributed denial-of-service incursions, and optimize page load speeds for high-resolution photography across mobile and desktop devices.

3. Lawful Basis and Concrete Purposes for Data Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and applicable international statutory frameworks, CorduroyFlight processes personal data exclusively under legitimate, lawful business justifications. Primarily, processing is mandatory for the execution of contractual and pre-contractual customer relationships, such as providing sizing advice for our A-2 and B-3 corduroy bombers, generating order invoices, and managing transit logistics with specialized luxury transport couriers. We never process personal details without an explicit, verifiable operational requirement.

Additionally, we process contact information under your freely given, informed consent when you opt into our email communications regarding newly milled 8-wale corduroy fabric batches, vintage aviation archive releases, and private showroom events at 181 Mercer Street. You maintain the autonomous right to revoke this consent instantaneously at any time by selecting the dedicated one-click unsubscribe mechanism integrated into every digital dispatch we transmit, or by emailing our privacy officer directly with your withdrawal request.

4. Data Storage, Cryptographic Security, and Retention Limits

All sensitive electronic communications, concierge inquiry transmissions, and database records are safeguarded utilizing modern cryptographic standards, including Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protocols and AES-256 bit data-at-rest encryption algorithms. Our internal network infrastructure is shielded by redundant web application firewalls, intrusion detection monitors, and strict multi-factor authentication requirements enforced across all atelier personnel with authorized access to customer communication portals. Physical paper measurement records maintained in our New York cutting room are locked in fireproof archival vaults.

We adhere to strict data minimization principles and retain customer correspondence only for the minimum duration required to achieve the stated business purpose or comply with federal tax and commercial recordkeeping statutes. General sizing inquiries and sizing assistance correspondence are retained for a maximum of twenty-four months to assist with repeat tailoring orders, after which all digital logs are permanently purged through secure cryptographic overwriting protocols. Financial transactional records required under United States commercial statutes are archived securely for seven years before definitive certified destruction.

5. Non-Disclosure and Third-Party Service Provider Standards

CorduroyFlight maintains an unyielding corporate stance against the commercial monetization of patron information: we have never sold, rented, leased, or bartered customer data to third-party advertising brokers, behavioral tracking syndicates, or commercial lead generators. We share data strictly with trusted infrastructural partners who operate under rigorous data processing agreements and are legally prohibited from utilizing your personal information for any independent purpose outside our verified instructions.

These vetted service providers include secure enterprise cloud hosting infrastructure located within certified domestic facilities, encrypted email delivery systems for sending order confirmations, and premium global shipping couriers who receive solely the recipient address and contact telephone number necessary to ensure delivery. In extraordinary circumstances, we may disclose personal data if formally mandated by valid legal subpoenas, court orders, or statutory regulatory mandates issued by competent law enforcement bodies exercising lawful jurisdiction.

6. Global Privacy Rights, Data Access, and Contact Channels

Regardless of your geographic jurisdiction, CorduroyFlight extends comprehensive privacy prerogatives to all patrons. You possess the legal right to request formal confirmation regarding whether your personal information is stored within our databases, obtain a complete machine-readable copy of your personal data file, demand the correction of inaccurate contact records, or instruct our team to permanently expunge all non-statutory records associated with your identity under the universally recognized "Right to be Forgotten."

To exercise any of these privacy rights, or to submit formal inquiries regarding our data handling methodologies, please submit a written communication to our designated Data Protection Officer at privacy@corduroyflight.com. Alternatively, inquiries may be dispatched via registered postal mail to CorduroyFlight Atelier, Attention: Legal & Privacy Affairs, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or by telephoning our concierge desk directly at +1-888-777-5845 during standard atelier hours.

Atelier Heritage

Reviving the Golden Age of Aviation Outerwear

Headquartered at 181 Mercer Street in New York, CorduroyFlight crafts heirloom flight jackets combining military utility with architectural tailoring.

Our Tailoring Philosophy

Flight jackets are the most demanding category in outerwear tailoring. Every curve must permit full cockpit reaching motion, every pocket must be accessible when seated, and every seam must withstand freezing crosswinds.

By reviving 8-wale British corduroy and matching it with solid brass Talon hardware, Australian shearling, and diamond-quilted Primaloft insulation, CorduroyFlight creates jackets engineered for a lifetime of adventure.
